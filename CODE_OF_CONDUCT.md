# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Treat each other with respect, be patient with questions, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person. Explain disagreements with evidence and give others room to learn.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, deliberate intimidation and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. The same expectations apply in issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or limit participation when behavior violates these expectations. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and consider context, impact and repeated conduct.

Report abusive content through GitHub's reporting and moderation tools. For a concern that needs private discussion, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details. Do not share personal information or allegations publicly while requesting that channel.
